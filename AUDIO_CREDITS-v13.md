# Audio provenance

**Preserved real CC0 nature recordings** (v8): Soft Rain by visionear, Stream by jackthemurray, Forest by J.Zazvurek, Ocean Waves by florianreichelt. Source project: https://github.com/abhinandansharma/ambiently .

**Themed world music** (both CC0 as marked on the original OpenGameArt pages):
- Ancient Egypt: “Egyptian meditation music”, brandon75689, https://opengameart.org/content/egyptian-meditation-music (v312.ogg).
- Japan: “Jaoan”, Tozan, https://opengameart.org/content/jaoan (Jaoan_0.ogg).

The game's existing action sound effects are retained, with subtle accent variations by world. Original synth background *melodies* from earlier prototypes are not used.
