How to Play

The board always uses up to 6 columns and 7 rows for larger, clearer tiles.

Tap any free Mahjong tile to move it into one of the 4 slots above the board.

When two matching tiles enter the collection, they vanish in a burst of light and earn points. Build consecutive matches for COMBO rewards.

A tile is free when nothing covers it and either its left or right side is open. Tap any stored tile to return it to the board.

SHUFFLE rearranges remaining tile faces; HINT reveals a match; UNDO reverses an action; MAGIC instantly matches a collected tile with its twin. Don't fill all four slots with unmatched tiles!
