# Emerald Mahjong v8 — Nature Audio Credits

All **background audio** is taken from real-world field recordings, not synthesized/generated. The four AAC/M4A loops are redistributed from the [Ambiently open-source demo](https://github.com/abhinandansharma/ambiently/tree/main/demo/public/sounds). They are 30-second trimmed, edited, encoded loops of CC0-recorded sounds from Freesound. Background melody has been fully removed. Original in-game click/match **sound effects** remain separate from nature ambience.
